# Auto-Renewal Disclosure at Purchase

**MUST** present material auto-renewal terms — price, billing interval, and cancellation path — conspicuously adjacent to the purchase control, not behind a terms link.

**Why:** Negative-option statutes make undisclosed renewals unenforceable; California has treated renewals buried away from the buy button as unconditional gifts owed back as refunds, and the FTC Negative Option Rule targets the same pattern.
**Detect:** read the checkout or paywall copy: renewal price and interval must sit within or beside the CTA; confirm cancellation completes online without a mandatory phone call.
**References:** ROSCA, 15 U.S.C. §8402, https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/8402, California Bus. & Prof. Code §17600 et seq.
